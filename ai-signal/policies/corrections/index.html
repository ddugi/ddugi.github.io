Publication policy

Corrections and updates

Effective 18 September 2026. Accuracy concerns are reviewed against the underlying evidence. Submit the article URL, disputed statement, explanation, and supporting source through the repository’s issue tracker.

How changes are recorded

  • A typo or formatting repair may be corrected without a prominent note when meaning is unchanged.
  • A material factual correction identifies the error, the corrected information, and the update date on the article.
  • A clarification explains material wording added to prevent a reasonable misunderstanding.
  • New developments may be added as an update without rewriting the historical publication date.
  • Removal is exceptional and considered for legal, safety, privacy, or fundamental accuracy reasons. A notice remains where appropriate.

The Git repository retains a technical change history. That history supplements the reader-facing correction note; it does not replace it.

Responses and appeals

The responsible editor evaluates requests and may seek further evidence. Disagreement with analysis alone is not necessarily an error, but factual premises and undisclosed conflicts remain reviewable. A person materially affected by a story may request that a concise response be considered.
