# Contributing

This is a maintained publication and research repository. Open an issue before substantial work so editorial and technical scope can be agreed.

## Pull requests

1. Create a focused branch and keep unrelated changes separate.
2. Do not edit generated AI Signal HTML. Update `ai-signal/content/publication.json` or its builder.
3. Run `python3 ai-signal/scripts/build.py`, `python3 ai-signal/scripts/test_build.py`, and `python3 ai-signal/scripts/build.py --check`.
4. Describe sources, assumptions, AI assistance, third-party material, and validation performed.
5. Never submit secrets, confidential material, personal data without a lawful basis, or material you do not have the right to publish.

By submitting a contribution, you certify that you created it or have the right to submit it, and that the code may be distributed under the MIT License. Non-code editorial material is not accepted for republication without an explicit written licence grant from the contributor.

Publication remains at the maintainer's editorial discretion. Acceptance does not create employment, payment, endorsement, or a right to publication.
