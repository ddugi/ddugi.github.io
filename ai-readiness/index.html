How the model works.

Each of the five dimensions has equal weight. Index = 100 × sum of scores ÷ 15, rounded to the nearest integer. The score measures the evidence you report; it does not predict project success or authorize production deployment.

The lowest-scoring dimensions appear first in the improvement list. A high average can hide a critical gap: review every dimension, especially identity and access, before interpreting the overall number.

Background: NIST AI RMF 1.0. This compact questionnaire and its weights are original draft choices, not an implementation or endorsement of the NIST framework.

Evidence rubric and limitations

0 = absent or unknown; 1 = planned or informal; 2 = implemented with incomplete or inconsistent evidence; 3 = implemented with repeatable evidence and review. Self-reported inputs are unverified. Five broad dimensions cannot capture every organizational risk. Use results to start a review, not to replace one.
