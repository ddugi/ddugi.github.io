How the model works.

Levels are cumulative. Each dimension earns its level only when it scores 3 and every preceding dimension also scores 3. A gap at Level 2 caps the result at Level 1 even if later controls are evidenced. Level 0 means the foundational evidence gate is unmet.

The five gates cover desired state, reconciliation, guarded changes, recovery, and fleet improvement. This progression is defined by this lab; it is not an OpenGitOps certification.

Background: OpenGitOps principles describe declarative, versioned, automatically pulled, continuously reconciled state. Our scoring and additional maturity gates are independent design choices.

Evidence rubric and limitations

0 = absent or unknown; 1 = planned or informal; 2 = implemented with incomplete or inconsistent evidence; 3 = implemented with repeatable evidence and review. Self-reported inputs are unverified. Five broad dimensions cannot capture every organizational risk. Use results to start a review, not to replace one.
